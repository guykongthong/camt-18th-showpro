SE CAMT SHOWPRO — VISUAL ASSETS

ASSET DRAFT SHEET

Five pieces the CSS-only prototype is currently faking with gradients and box-shadows. Each tile below is a rough, working sketch of the direction — shape, palette, scale — not final art. Use them as a starting point for real illustration or texture work before drop-in.

CRT Static Noise
Loop · 48×36px source · pixelated scale
Fills the TV screen during the "static" phase of the power-on sequence, and sits behind each cabinet's screen bezel at low opacity. Export as a 1–2s looping WebM/APNG, or keep it live like this canvas draft.
Arcade Carpet
Seamless tile · SVG or 240×240 PNG
Replaces the flat radial-gradient hall background with the jagged geometric carpet every real arcade shares. Tiles edge-to-edge behind the project grid.
Cabinet Chassis
Front view · SVG · ~5:7 aspect
A fully illustrated cabinet body — marquee, screen bezel, joystick, buttons, kick plate — to replace ArcadeCabinet's current gradient-and-shadow box.
Zone Badges
4× 8×8px sprites · one per zone accent
AI · WEB · MOBILE · IOT — small pixel-art glyphs to replace the plain colored dot next to each project's zone label and in the zone nav.
Insert-Coin Token
8×8px sprite · optional 3-frame flip sheet
Sits on the INSERT COIN button and drives a short token-drop animation on click, before the cabinet screen zooms into the project detail.

None of this is final art — it's scaffolding so the direction is obvious before anyone spends time in Illustrator/Aseprite. The carpet and cabinet are built from plain rects/polygons here; the zone badges and coin are literal pixel bitmaps (an array of 1s and 0s), which is the easiest format to hand off to redraw at a larger resolution.
